Four negative cases are pinned (not fixed / too narrow / too short / our own node). |
| Also for #18 (reflow triggers) | Fixed | Three triggers share one watcher: `window.resize`, a `ResizeObserver` on the composer card, and a `MutationObserver` on `document.body` with childList + subtree + attributes filtered to class/style/hidden. The editable-node selector now tolerates an empty value and plaintext-only, not only the literal true. |
